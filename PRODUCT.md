# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The presenter operates a laptop and assigns a phone role to issuer treasury. Three audience participants act as fund operations, bank operations, and presenter takeover as needed. They use the product together in a meeting room with no accounts, email, or wallet installation.

## Product Purpose

Reserve Operations Lab is a fictional, interactive issuer reserve operations demonstration. It shows that a fund request can be accepted and processed while proceeds are still not available bank cash. The product makes responsibilities, pending transactions, confirmations, balances, and exceptions visible across shared devices. The success condition is an accurate, understandable walkthrough of the six-step Friday redemption and its exceptions, with no false claim about settlement speed, savings, or blockchain performance.

## Brand Personality

Professional, restrained, clear. Explain financial operations with short, consistent sentences using ASD-STE100 guidance. The visual language supports a live presentation, not crypto trading or investment promotion.

## Anti-references

- No hacker-terminal or crypto trading aesthetic.
- No artificial slowness in conventional systems or speed claims for shared-ledger mode.
- No invented savings, performance, real Vanguard product, or endorsement claims.
- No implied real bank connection, wallet, blockchain settlement, or movement of money through the ledger.
- No oversized promotional metrics, card grids, gradients, or decorative particles.

## Design Principles

- Make each actor, next action, and unresolved item explicit.
- Keep balances authoritative and never count pending proceeds as available cash.
- Show workflow evidence without suggesting the ledger owns financial records.
- Give each participant one clear task and a usable phone interface.
- Keep the financial simulation transparent on every screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard access, reduced motion, readable contrast in both themes, clear labels and states, minimum 44px touch targets, and usable content at 200% text enlargement. Color alone must not communicate status.
