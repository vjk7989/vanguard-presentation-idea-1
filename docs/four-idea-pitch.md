# Four-idea pitch walkthrough

This is a fictional, public, educational demonstration. The QR keeps everyone in one room. Ask participants to pick the named job on their phone; use admin takeover for any unclaimed job. The presenter workflow animates **accepted clicks**, not sample records. Each role's Overview, Work, and Activity views show its own queue and evidence trail. The sample feed is background context; it is not a real bank, market, tax, or blockchain transaction.

| Idea | Say this while showing the projector | Ask these desks to click |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Friday reserves** | A fund needs cash for redemptions; each organization sees its own instruction and confirmation. | Issuer Treasury requests the $150m redemption; Fund Operations accepts and processes it; Bank Payments confirms proceeds; Issuer Treasury approves the $450m payout; Bank Payments confirms payout. Try the delayed-bank exception before confirmation. |
| **2 · Securities recall** | A Portfolio Manager sold **400,000 shares** for T+1. Only **150,000** are free; **250,000** are on loan. A broker's notice is not custody receipt. | Portfolio Manager places sale → Lending Officer recalls **150,000** from Broker A and **100,000** from Broker B → both Stock Loan Officers notify returns → Custody Settlement Officer confirms each receipt → Custody confirms all **400,000** are deliverable. For the exception, have Broker B flag a delay, then let Custody report the **100,000-share shortfall** before Broker B returns and Custody confirms. |
| **3 · Currency hedge** | Fund and dealer initially value the **€400m** forward differently: **$1.80m vs $1.83m**. Agreement, payment, and booking are distinct facts. | Hedge Operations records the 4 pm snapshot and sends its valuation → Dealer Margin flags the difference → Hedge Operations agrees the $1.80m basis → Dealer Margin issues the call → Paying Bank confirms the simulated payment → Fund Accounting books it. Pause after call issuance to show **pending**, not paid. |
| **4 · Tax proof** | A Dutch pension scheme receives a fictional **€1m** French dividend. Evidence must be current before the depositary can submit relief at source. | Pension Scheme Tax Officer requests proof → admin simulates authority issuance → Tax Compliance verifies it → Depositary submits the claim. For the exception, admin issues expired proof, Tax Compliance flags it, Pension Officer requests renewal, admin issues replacement proof, and Tax Compliance verifies it. Claim submission remains **pending** until an explicit, presenter-only synthetic confirmation. |

The professional desks vary by scenario. Idea 2 has five roles and Idea 3 has four; three audience phones still work because the presenter may act for any unclaimed role. The three-dot switcher changes the active idea for everyone without a new QR or login. Returning to an idea restores its run; Reset intentionally starts a fresh run for **that idea only**.

The “Without blockchain” view shows separate fictional party records and reconciliation; “With blockchain” shows the **same accepted events** in one simulated, SHA-256-linked history. It is not a live chain, proof of settlement speed, guarantee of delivery, real tax-authority decision, or real financial integration. Do not present any fixed tax rate or relief amount; none is specified by the deck.
