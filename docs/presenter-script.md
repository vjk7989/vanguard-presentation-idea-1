# Three-minute presenter script

**0:00–0:30 — Set the scene**
Select **Enter admin dashboard**, then **Show QR**. Ask participants to scan the permanent `DEMO01` link; their devices appear in the live count before they choose issuer, fund, or bank. A participant can use **Change role**. If needed, use **Kick** on the device roster; that person can scan again or press **Rejoin**.

"Harbor Dollar is a fictional issuer. It has $2bn in obligations, $300m in bank cash, and $1.7bn in assumed eligible fund holdings. Customers request $450m in bank payouts. The issuer requests $150m from the fund to cover the gap. This is a simulated workflow, not a real reserve attestation."

**0:30–1:15 — Request and process**
Ask Issuer to open a **Practice coordination** request. Its device action appears on the projector as a dashed Issuer → Fund path. Have Fund review it, then Bank acknowledge back to Issuer. Repeat if time permits; each case has a separate reference and never moves cash. Briefly open one **Sample desk item** to contrast a fictional background record with accepted live coordination. Then point everyone to the highlighted **Friday main action**.

"Issuer treasury requests cash. Fund operations accepts the request. Notice that bank cash remains $300m. The fund then processes the redemption: fund holdings fall to $1.55bn and $150m becomes pending proceeds. Pending means the bank has not confirmed receipt."

**1:15–1:55 — Confirm and pay**
"Bank operations confirms the incoming $150m. Available cash now reaches $450m. Issuer treasury can approve $450m of payouts. The bank confirms completion. Cash closes at $0 and obligations at $1.55bn. We do not claim confirmed 1:1 backing while bank verification is pending."

**1:55–2:30 — Exceptions**
"On another run, I can delay bank confirmation after fund processing. The $150m stays pending, and payout approval stays blocked. I can also repeat the same bank notice after receipt. The original reference is recognized, so cash does not increase again."

**2:30–3:00 — Compare records**
"The conventional view shows separate illustrative issuer, fund, and bank records with reconciliation. The simulated shared workflow history shows the exact same accepted events with SHA-256 links. The bank and fund remain authoritative for their own records. Cash does not move through the ledger. This demo shows the proposed workflow and control points. It does not prove that blockchain outperforms conventional automation."
